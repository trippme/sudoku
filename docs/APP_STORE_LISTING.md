## Description (4000 chars max)
```
Sudoku, done right — clean, fast, and free of ads and clutter.

Every puzzle plays entirely on your device, so you can solve anywhere, online or
off. When you want a challenge, sign in with just your name and email to climb
the global leaderboard and go head-to-head with friends on the exact same puzzle.

FEATURES
• Four difficulty levels, from relaxed to genuinely tough
• Thousands of unique puzzles, each with one guaranteed solution
• Fully offline — no connection needed to play
• No ads, no pop-ups, no paywalls
• Smart hints that teach the next logical step, not just the answer
• Mistake tracking and a clean, distraction-free board
• Pencil marks / notes for working through candidates
• Light and dark themes that are easy on the eyes
• Detailed stats: best times, hints used, and streaks

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
• Send any puzzle to a friend and see who solves it faster
• Get notified the moment a friend finishes a shared game
• A global leaderboard ranked by fewest hints, then fastest time
• Your email is only ever shared with friends you add — never shown publicly

PRIVACY FIRST
The app works completely offline and sends nothing to our servers until you
choose to use the online features. No ads. No trackers. No selling your data.
Read our full policy at https://the949dude.com/sudoku/privacy.html

Download Sudoku and start solving.
```
